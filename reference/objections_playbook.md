# Objection Playbook

"I already have a receptionist."
Denty protects her. It handles the calls she cannot reach, after hours or when she is with a patient.

"Is this a scam?"
Try the live demo. No forms, no card. Talk to Denty and decide.

"Why $2,000 upfront?"
That covers call routing, calendar connection, and testing for your workflow. If Denty books zero after hours appointments in 30 days, the setup fee is refunded.

"We are too small for this."
Smaller practices feel a missed call more, not less. One recovered patient covers the setup cost.

"Why do I need my own Vapi account?"
It keeps usage billing transparent and in your name. Denty is designed for your workflow. Setup and management stay with Outreach Studio.

"Why pay you monthly if I already pay Vapi?"
Vapi is the usage bill. The monthly fee covers monitoring, small updates, and fixes if something breaks.

"How do I know it is actually working?"
Screen-share verification. Every booking shows a timestamp and an ID, visible in your own calendar.
