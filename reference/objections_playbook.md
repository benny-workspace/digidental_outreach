# Objection Playbook

"We already handle this internally."
Position the offer as support for gaps the current process does not cover, not a replacement for the team.

"Is this legitimate?"
Offer a low-friction proof step: a short walkthrough, a live demo, or a clear explanation of the workflow.

"Why does it cost this much?"
Tie price to setup work, measurable outcome, ongoing support, and risk reduction. Do not invent guarantees unless the Admin profile includes one.

"Now is not the right time."
Acknowledge timing, leave the door open, and offer to reconnect when the problem becomes a priority.

"Send me more information."
Send the shortest useful proof: what it does, who it is for, what changes after setup, and the next step.
